The Story

Started with one kettle

Kahwa House began the way most good kahwa does — over a single copper kettle, shared between neighbours on a cold evening. What started as a small counter with three stools has grown into a room where students, shopkeepers, and old friends still trade the same seats every week.

We keep the recipe close to how it was passed down: saffron threads bloomed slowly, cinnamon bark rather than powder, and almonds cracked the same morning they're served. Nothing here is built for speed. It's built for the second cup.

"A good kahwa isn't rushed. Neither is a good conversation."
